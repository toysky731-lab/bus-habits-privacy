常見問題

點解有時個時間唔準？

App 唔會自己估。所有時間都係直接由營運商經 data.gov.hk 資料一線通 出嘅實時到站資料攞返嚟，同你喺站頭電子牌見到嗰個係同一份。塞車、改道、車壞， 營運商幾時更新，App 就幾時跟。

如果成條路線都冇數，多數係嗰邊暫時收唔到車，唔係你部機壞。

點解冇時間表？

因為公開資料入面根本冇。呢啲 API 只出「下一班幾時到」，冇出「平日每 12 分鐘一班」 嗰種班次表。與其自己砌個估算出嚟扮準，不如唔出。

搵唔到我要嗰條線

而家支援九巴、城巴、專線綠色小巴、港鐵重鐵。輕鐵、電車、渡輪、紅色小巴 未支援。紅色小巴冇官方實時資料，做唔到。

如果係上面四種而搵唔到，請電郵話我知條線同方向，我會查。

通知冇出

逐項查：

  • iOS 設定 → 巴士習慣 → 通知，有冇開；
  • App 內 設定 →「邊個習慣要通知」，嗰個習慣有冇著；
  • 今日係咪公眾假期 —— 開咗假期唔通知嘅習慣，假期當日係唔會響嘅；
  • 今日係咪嗰個習慣嘅指定星期。

通知係喺你設定嘅時間前一段時間響，唔係即時到站提示。

換咗新 iPhone，習慣會唔會冇咗？

只要兩部機都登入同一個 iCloud 帳戶並開咗 iCloud Drive，習慣同路線會自己跟住過去。 冇登入 iCloud 嘅話，資料淨係留喺原本嗰部機。

打賞退款

打賞係經 App Store 處理，我收唔到你嘅付款資料，亦都退唔到款。 退款要向 Apple 申請：reportaproblem.apple.com。

點刪除我啲資料？

冇帳戶，所以冇嘢要「登出」。刪走個 App 就會清走機上嘅資料； iCloud 嗰份要喺 iOS 設定 → 你個名 → iCloud → 管理帳戶儲存空間 入面刪。

English

Data sources and assets

The app has no server of ours. Live times come straight from data.gov.hk (KMB, Citybus, green minibus, MTR). The MTR line and station table comes from MTR's open data and ships inside the app. Public holidays come from 1823. The route-number typeface is Fredoka, SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose licence file ships with the app.

Because the app connects to those services directly, they can see your device's IP address. See the privacy policy.

Contact

Email amigo.choi@fano.ai. Include your device model, iOS version, and which route or stop was wrong — it makes a real difference.

Why are the times sometimes off?

The app does not estimate anything. Every time comes straight from the operators through data.gov.hk — the same feed as the electronic sign at the stop. If traffic, a diversion or a breakdown delays a bus, the app is as current as the operator is.

Why is there no timetable?

The open data does not contain one. These APIs publish "next arrival", not a schedule. Inventing an estimate and presenting it as a timetable would look more useful and be less true.

My route is missing

Supported: KMB, Citybus, green minibus, and MTR heavy rail. Light Rail, trams, ferries and red minibuses are not — red minibuses publish no real-time data at all. If it is one of the four and still missing, email me the route and direction.

Notifications are not arriving

Check iOS Settings → Bus Habits → Notifications; then in the app, Settings → which habits have notifications on; then whether today is a public holiday (habits set to stay quiet on holidays will); then whether today is one of that habit's weekdays.

I got a new iPhone

Signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on, your habits and routes follow you. Without iCloud, everything stays on the original device.

Refunds for tips

Tips go through the App Store. I never see your payment details and cannot issue refunds — ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

There are no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of. Deleting the app removes what is on the device; the iCloud copy is removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. See the privacy policy.
