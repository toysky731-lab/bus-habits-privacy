私隱政策 · 最後更新 2026 年 8 月 31 日

收集乜

冇帳戶，冇登入。你嘅習慣、路線同設定存喺你部機，開咗 iCloud 就存喺你自己嘅 iCloud，我睇唔到。

App 會經 PostHog 收匿名使用統計（例如開過 App、搵過幾次車），連同一個匿名裝置編號。呢啲數據唔會連繫到你嘅身分，亦唔會用嚟追蹤你。App 唔會送出你打嘅字、站名或者習慣名。

因為 App 直接向 data.gov.hk 同 1823 攞資料，呢啲服務會見到你部機嘅 IP 位址。

刪除

刪走 App 就清走機上資料。iCloud 嗰份喺 iOS 設定 → 你個名 → iCloud 度刪。

兒童

App 唔會刻意收集 13 歲以下人士嘅資料。

English

No accounts, no sign-in. Your habits, routes and settings stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if iCloud is on. I cannot see them.

The app sends anonymous usage statistics through PostHog, along with an anonymous device identifier. This is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. Text you type, stop names and habit names are never sent.

Because the app calls data.gov.hk and 1823 directly, those services can see your device's IP address.

Deleting the app removes local data; the iCloud copy is removed in iOS Settings. The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
